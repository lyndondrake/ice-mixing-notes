And one finding that was not registered and is the substantive answer to “the slow even modes at (1,2,2) are a different object and nobody has said what”:

The slowest even mode is the emergent magnetic helicity. It is a pseudoscalar — its character under the cell’s 256-element affine automorphism group is exactly det(M_g) (max deviation 2.7e-14) — and the single observable h = Im(P_z(q_y)* P_x(q_y)) + Im(P_x(q_z)* P_y(q_z)), the transverse part of q·(P* × P) at q_min, has overlap 0.75859 with it. The span of the eleven independent helicity columns captures 0.83456 of the mode — essentially all of its degree-2 content (0.85322). Every even member of the production panel has overlap below 1e-25 with it, i.e. exactly zero by symmetry. The practical consequence: the exact tau_exp of that one helicity observable is 389.45 attempts against a true 1/Δ of 389.95, where the best existing panel member reaches 263.13 and the odd polarisation reaches 242.07. Adding a pseudoscalar member to icemix/panel.py closes the parity blind spot at this cell for no extra Markov time. See §4 and §8.6.

2. The (1,2,2) two-parity spectra

The giant component is closed under global arrow reversal R, which acts on it as a free involution (no bit-string equals its complement) with 110,712 orbits. 3. Characterisation of v_even

(a) Momentum

translation character on v_even trace character on the slowest odd multiplet (deg 4)
T_y (one cell in y) 1.000000000000 −0.0
T_z (one cell in z) 1.000000000000 +0.0
T_yT_z 1.000000000000 −4.0

v_even is at zero transverse momentum — H4 TRUE.

The odd multiplet’s traces are the genuine, basis-independent characters (the individual <u, T u> are properties of an arbitrary Lanczos basis, not of the chain). Trace 0 for each generator and −4 for their product forces the four-dimensional multiplet to consist of two states with (χ_y, χ_z) = (−1,+1) and two with (+1,−1). Since b = c = 2, q_min along a transverse axis is the zone boundary and e^{iq_min·a} = −1, so those are exactly the momenta of P_z(q_y) and P_x/y(q_z): the slowest odd modes sit at the transverse zone-boundary momenta, and the slowest even mode at zero momentum.

(b) Point group — derived, not assumed

icemix.parity.lattice_automorphisms derives the cell’s affine automorphisms from the coordinates: candidates p ↦ M p + t (mod box) with M a signed axis permutation preserving the box lattice, accepted only if the map permutes the site set, carries every bond to a bond, and carries the derived hexagon set to itself. Where the map exchanges the A and B sublattices the arrow bit complements, so the induced state map is s ↦ π(s) ⊕ f with an explicit flip mask; that composition law is unit-tested against repeated application.

Two crystallographic anchors were asserted, not assumed:

  • at (1,1,1) the construction returns 192 automorphisms — exactly the number of general positions of Fd-3m in one conventional cubic cell, with 96 sublattice-swapping (a test);
  • at (1,2,2) it returns 256 (16 point operations preserving the (4,8,8) box × 16 FCC-coset translations), all 256 of which act on the giant component — i.e. the giant component is a union of full symmetry orbits.

Every automorphism was verified to commute with P on the sector (a test at (1,1,2)).

The result. χ(g) = <v_even, g v_even> is ±1 on every one of the 256 elements, and

max_g | chi(g) - det(M_g) |  =  2.66e-14        (128 proper, 128 improper)

so v_even transforms in the determinant (pseudoscalar) representation: invariant under all 128 proper operations, sign-reversing under all 128 improper ones. Deviation from the trivial character is 2.0 (i.e. it is not totally symmetric); the stabiliser has exactly 128 elements. The second and third even modes are neither pseudoscalar nor trivial (deviation 2.0 from both) — they carry other one-dimensional characters. This is developed in §4.

(c) Degree structure

Least-squares projection onto the span of spin monomials σ_S = ∏_{b∈S}(2x_b − 1) under the uniform measure on the giant component (the monomials are not orthogonal once restricted to an ice sector, so this is a genuine Gram solve, done by symmetric eigendecomposition with an explicit rank cutoff). σ_S has parity (−1)^{|S|}, so an even vector has exactly zero overlap with every odd-degree monomial — measured 8.7e-20, asserted.

Degree 3 (41,664 columns) and degree 4 (635,376 columns) were not run; costs and reasons in §9.

The plan’s §4(c) expected the flippability overlaps to be tiny; they are in fact exactly zero, by symmetry — every one of these observables is a true scalar under improper operations and v_even is a pseudoscalar. That is a stronger statement than “suppressed by powers of q_min²”, and it is not a finite-size effect: no series length and no system size makes a symmetry-forbidden overlap appear.

(e) Degeneracy and splitting

v_even is simple, split from the second even mode by 1.229e-3 in λ and from the third by 1.275e-3. The even sector’s first degenerate multiplet is 4-fold at λ = 0.9942156099 (rank 5–8). The odd sector’s slowest is 4-fold at λ = 0.9958774927 and its second is 4-fold at 0.9954992346. So no near-degeneracy threatens the identification of v_even.

The pair amplitude

Since v_even is 85 % degree 2, its degree-2 content is described by the symmetric 64 × 64 matrix C_ij = <v_even, σ_i σ_j>. Its diagonal vanishes identically (σ_i² = 1 and the eigenvector is orthogonal to the constant) — measured 1.5e-18. C is not rank one: effective rank ‖C‖_F⁴/Σw⁴ = 18.35, top eigenvalue weight 0.0756, and the leading eigenvalues come in ± pairs (−0.002933, −0.002933, +0.002933, +0.002933), which is the signature of an antisymmetric bilinear pairing rather than the square of one form. The six leading eigenvectors each overlap 0.46–0.48 with a P_j(q) component and 0.24–0.47 with an odd eigenvector’s spin profile, and each puts a quarter of its weight on the bonds crossing one cut plane. That is exactly the structure of Im(P_i* P_j) — §4.

4. Unexpected: the mode is the emergent magnetic helicity

This was not registered; it fell out of the character analysis and was then tested directly.

For a divergence-free field the natural bilinear pseudoscalar is q · (P* × P): with q along axis k and (i,j,k) cyclic,

h_k(q) = Im( P_i(q)* P_j(q) )
       = Re P_i · Im P_j  -  Im P_i · Re P_j .

It is even under R (a product of two odd factors) and changes sign under every improper lattice operation (the cross product does) — the same transformation law the character analysis found for v_even, and one that nothing in the existing panel has. In the continuum this is the magnetic helicity ∫ A·B of the emergent gauge field.

Measured overlaps with v_even, and with the second even mode as a control:

helicity observable overlap v_even overlap 2nd even mode
h_y + h_z at q_min (helicity_yz) 0.75859 1.5e-25
h_y at q_min 0.43300 2.6e-27
h_z at q_min 0.43300 4.0e-25
h_x + h_y + h_z at q_min 0.42149 7.9e-26
h_y + h_z at 2q_min 0.27991 5.5e-26
h_y − h_z at q_min (staggered) 2.3e-27 2.7e-25
h_x at q_min (thin axis) 0.03464 8.5e-27

The span of the eleven independent helicity columns captures 0.83456 of v_even — against a total degree-2 content of 0.85322. In other words the helicity family accounts for 98 % of everything degree-2 in the mode. It captures 4.7e-22 of the second even mode (which is therefore a different object entirely) and 0.81703 of the third even mode.

The symmetry bookkeeping closes on itself, which is the check that this is not a coincidence. The y ↔︎ z swap [[1,0,0],[0,0,1],[0,1,0]] has det = −1, so it must send v_even → −v_even; geometrically it exchanges h_y ↔︎ h_z and flips the helicity’s sign, so h_y + h_z → −(h_y + h_z) (odd, matches) while h_y − h_z → +(h_y − h_z) (even, must be orthogonal). Measured overlap of h_y − h_z with v_even: 2.3e-27, i.e. exactly zero. Likewise h_x, the helicity about the thin axis, is nearly decoupled (0.0346) — there is only one cell of room along x, so there is no long-wavelength helicity to be had there.

So the helicity observable’s tau_exp is the true relaxation time, to the last digit, while the whole existing panel tops out at 263.13 (the second even mode) or 242.07 (the slowest odd mode) — short of the gap by a factor 1.61. F(q), the panel’s designated even member, couples to no mode in the top 28 of either parity: its rho(t) decays on a fast local scale (tau_int = 0.78 sweeps), exactly the “invisible slow tail” the plan’s §4(c) anticipated, but for a symmetry reason rather than an amplitude one.

What this says about the thin-cell pathology. The three slow even modes at (1,2,2) are not two-photon states of the Gaussian argument; they are long-wavelength helicity (chirality) fluctuations of the emergent field, which the plan’s §4 argument does not contain and which no member of the production panel can couple to. The ladder of §6 supports the reading that they need two thick directions: the quasi-1D cells, which have only one, put the even sector at Δ_even/Δ_odd ≈ 3.7–3.9 instead.

What it does not say. This is a statement about a 64-spin cell of thickness 1. It does not establish that a helicity mode exists, or is slow, in cubic geometry; §2 of the plan already records that exact diagonalisation cannot reach cubic cells. What it does establish is that the reason the panel missed the slow mode at (1,2,2) is a symmetry the panel does not contain, and that adding one pseudoscalar member fixes it — see §8.6.

5. The odd control

The slowest odd eigenvalue is 4-fold degenerate, so all four readings below are multiplet invariants; the per-mode degree-1 fractions agree to 1.2e-13, which is the check that the quantity is basis-independent.

  • degree-1 fraction: 0.597232 (> 0.5 — asserted, PASS);
  • the four largest single-observable projections onto the multiplet are Pz_qy_re, Pz_qy_im, Py_qz_re, Py_qz_im — the transverse polarisation at q_min — each 0.597232;
  • the span of the eight transverse P(q_min) components captures 0.5972318685 of each multiplet member, identical to the degree-1 fraction to 10 digits. So the entire degree-1 content of the slowest odd multiplet is the transverse polarisation at q_min, with nothing left over. That is the sharpest form of the control the brief asked for, and it passes.

The remaining 40 % of the odd mode is degree ≥ 3 and was not decomposed further (§9).

(Inference, not measurement: a ratio near 4 is what a single quantum at 2 q_min would give under ω ∝ q², where two quanta at ±q_min would give 2; this report does not test that.)

Design for a later dedicated run

  1. Enumerator. A C extension in the style of icemix/_msc.c, built through the same ctypes path: the same DFS with constraint propagation and the winding-slack pruning of enumerate_ice_states, with the state carried in two uint64 words, the Schedule passed in from Python, and leaves written into a caller-preallocated (N, 2) uint64 buffer (never a Python list — 196 M Python ints alone is ~7.8 GiB). Expect ~100× the Python rate, so minutes. Gates: (a) the leaf count must equal frontier_count’s 196,465,480 exactly; (b) at (1,2,2) the C enumerator’s sorted output must equal enumerate_ice_states(zero_winding_only=True) bit-for-bit.
  2. Index. Not np.searchsorted — 1.91 µs/lookup is 24 cache misses per lookup and costs 10 h on its own. Lexsort the (N,2) array once, then build an open-addressing hash table of ~2^29 slots (2.1 GiB, int32 payload) keyed on a mix of the two words. Target ~100 ns/lookup → ~31 min for the whole adjacency build.
  3. Adjacency. CSR with implicit uniform weight (no data array): int32 indices, int64 indptr, built hexagon by hexagon in a count pass then a fill pass. 13.5 GiB.
  4. Mat-vec. A small C/OpenMP kernel for y = (1 − n_flip/(2 n_hex)) x + (1/(2 n_hex)) A x over that CSR. It is gather-bound on a 786 MB vector; re-measure the DRAM-scale random-gather rate before committing — the 10–40 ns/nnz bracket above is the largest uncertainty in the whole projection.
  5. Parity. Do not materialise P_even/P_odd at this size. Run the solver on P with each iterate projected, x ← (x ± x[R])/2, which needs only the R index permutation (int32, 786 MB) and one extra gather per iteration.
  6. Eigensolver. Plain Lanczos needs O(10³) mat-vecs because the top of the spectrum is clustered within 1e-3 of 1. Use LOBPCG with a small block and a Chebyshev polynomial accelerator on P, or a polynomial-filtered subspace iteration; shift-invert is out (no factorisation at this size).
  7. Operations. Checkpoint the enumeration output, the CSR, and the solver basis to local NVMe — a 20 h run must survive. Submit through expq with memory_limit (32 GiB) and a canonical ProgressReporter feed carrying a per-mat-vec step counter, per the experiment conventions.

Note also that (1,2,3) still has thickness 1 in x, so — per plan §2 — it would test the thin-cell pathology, not cubic geometry. Given §4 and §6, the specific prediction a (1,2,3) run would test is whether the helicity mode stays slowest when one transverse direction is lengthened, i.e. whether the ratio moves back toward the quasi-1D value or stays below 1. That is a sharper target than the ratio-toward-2 reading as registered.

8. Defects and deviations recorded

  1. pyrun.sh is unusable in this repository. It refuses with “no .venv/ found (searched from ~/repo/project/ice-mixing to /)”. The project has no virtualenv; stages 1–3 were run under the system interpreter (~/.pyenv/shims/python3, 3.12.13, numpy 1.26.4, scipy 1.17.1) and this run was too. Not a defect in the project, but the brief’s instruction could not be followed as written and the deviation is recorded rather than hidden.

  2. exptop is not importable from the system interpreter. It lives in its own venv at ~/repo/project/exptop/. grep -rl "class ProgressReporter" --include='*.py' . | grep -v .venv over this repository returns nothing — there is no vendored copy here, and none was created. Stages 1–3 used a hand-rolled heartbeat writing results/progress.json, which is outside the exptop feed root and therefore invisible to exptop. Rather than vendor anything, the run appends ~/repo/project/exptop/src to sys.path and uses the canonical exptop.progress.ProgressReporter alongside the repo-local heartbeat; results.json records which it got (meta.canonical_progress_feed = "canonical"). If exptop is absent the run degrades to the local heartbeat and says so.

  3. Per-writer temp filenames. The repo’s stage-1 Progress class has the heartbeat thread and the main thread writing the same progress.json.tmp, which races on os.replace and can kill the daemon thread silently. This run’s copy uses per-writer temp names and wraps all progress I/O in try/except. The stage-1/2/3 scripts still carry the racy version; not fixed here (they are historical artefacts of completed stages) but recorded for the main loop.

  4. Basis-dependence trap, caught. The first pass reported per-eigenvector overlaps for the slowest odd mode. That eigenvalue is 4-fold degenerate, so those numbers are properties of an arbitrary Lanczos basis, not of the chain — and indeed they moved between two runs (0.3887 → 0.3166) while the physics did not. Every odd reading was converted to a multiplet invariant (trace character; projection onto the invariant subspace) and the per-mode spread is now reported as evidence (1.2e-13).

  5. Two zombie exptop feed cards — my defect. Runs 2 and 3 of the ledger in §1 were stopped with tmux kill-session -t stage4d while the script was being extended. tmux kill-session delivers SIGHUP, and the canonical ProgressReporter installs handlers for SIGTERM and SIGINT only, so its exit path never ran and the cards ice-mixing-stage4d-1246242 and ice-mixing-stage4d-1253319 were left reading status: running against dead PIDs — they show in exptop as hung jobs. Neither a SIGKILL nor a hang was involved; it was SIGHUP, which the reporter cannot catch as configured. The main loop stamped both failed. Corrective practice adopted for the rest of this task: stop a run with kill -TERM <pid> (or tmux send-keys C-c) and wait, never kill-session; stamp the card by hand after any deliberate kill; and open no feed at all for sub-minute probes (preflight.py opens none). No handler was added to run_thin_even.py — the reporter’s own SIGTERM/SIGINT handlers are correct and duplicating them would be worse. Upstream note for exptop: a SIGHUP handler on the same exit path would have prevented this entirely, since tmux kill-session and terminal loss are the ordinary ways a detached run dies.

  6. Actionable for the project: the production panel (icemix/panel.py) contains no pseudoscalar. At (1,2,2) every even member is exactly orthogonal to the slowest mode, so the panel’s “slowest of the panel” is a lower bound that misses the gap by a factor 1.61 there for a reason no amount of statistics or series length fixes. Adding h = Im(P_z(q_y)* P_x(q_y)) + Im(P_x(q_z)* P_y(q_z)) — three multiplies and two subtractions on numbers measure_panel already records — closes it: the exact tau_exp of that observable at (1,2,2) is 389.45 attempts against a true 1/Δ of 389.95, where the best existing member reaches 263.13. It costs no extra Markov time (it is a function of the polarisation components already buffered) and it is even, so it complements rather than replaces P⊥. Whether the cubic panel has the same blind spot is untested — this report cannot say, and §4 is explicit about that; the cheap way to find out is to add the member and compare, since a cubic tau(h) exceeding tau(P⊥) would be a finding on its own.
