Theorem U′ certified at sides seven and eight and on four cells that are not cubic; the counterexample search

Theorem U′ holds on the cubic cells of side 7 and 8 and on (3,3,9), (4,4,8), (3,3,12) and (6,6,9): 224 whole-torus queries (16 per rod axis: two sublattices, two orders of the transverse types, two positions of each bridgehead; all three axes on the cells that are not cubic, where the forty-eight hypotheses fall into three orbits of the formula’s symmetries), every one unsatisfiable with a satisfiable control; 155 DRAT proofs checked by drat-trim (every query on the three small cells, one per orbit on (6,6,9), one per sublattice and order at sides seven and eight, the cyclic permutation of the axes being a checked automorphism).
