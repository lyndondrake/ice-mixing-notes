theorem-h-orientation / statement-r-box-lemma

Statement R and its box lemma (Conjecture \(R_b\); Theorem U section, item 8)

A weaker relative of (V): two aligned rods extend to a whole line of their type; its first step certified for \(b\) from 3 to 6.

Two rods of type \(k\) four apart, a lower bridgehead of type \(a\) and the upper bridgehead on the other side not of type \(a\) make the whole line of the rods of type \(k\). Its first step is forced inside a box over one closed transverse circle, \(x \in [-3, 2b + 1]\), \(|z| \leq 2b - 1\), with the \(y\)-circle of the cell whole, whatever \(a\), \(c\) and \(\gcd(b, c)\).
