theorem-h-orientation / thm-14

Theorem 14 (cells with a coprime pair of extents), with Lemmas 8 to 10 and Theorems 12, 13, 15

Theorem H proved by hand on every cell with a pair of coprime extents.

On a cell in which some pair of extents is coprime, every odd frozen state lacks one of the three types, so Theorem H holds.
