theorem-h-orientation / closed-kink-counts

The kink counts per class cannot close (V) (Theorem U section, item 10)

By solver on four cells with controls, the pattern of (V) makes the defect one constant on every chain of a three-grid window, non-negative on \([-3, 2g - 2]\), and with the negation zero on \([-3, 2g - 1]\) and not before, so given the pattern on the torus (V) is equivalent to a positive defect, with the same reach \(2g + 1\). Two parallel unit sheets of one sense through the two rods with defect zero satisfy the wave equation, the summed law \(P^k\), Lemma 6, the bounds, the pins of the pattern and the negation round the whole torus of each cell, and every enumerated solution short of the threshold extends by the wave equation round the rod axis within the bounds. The obstruction lies in which positions inside a class are kinked, not in how many.
