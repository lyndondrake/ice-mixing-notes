theorem-h-orientation / thin-cells-aperiodic

Frozen zero-flux states with no translation symmetry on thin cells

On the cells \((1,1,2)\), \((1,2,2)\), \((1,2,3)\), \((1,3,3)\), \((1,3,4)\) and \((1,4,5)\) there are frozen zero-flux states of even curl, with all six directions, fixed by no nonzero translation. On \((1,1,1)\) there is none.

Solver witnesses, each re-checked by an independent program (ice, frozen, flux zero, trivial stabiliser); the \((1,2,2)\) witness run again by the main loop. This refutes the periodicity conclusion on thin cells and shows that Theorem P’s hypothesis that every extent is at least two cannot be dropped.
