theorem-h-orientation / closed-r-then-monodromy

Statement R, a monodromy in the weights, and the heights are not a route to (V) (Theorem U section, item 9)

Statement R assumed on the whole rod line shortens the reach of (V) from \(2g + 1\) to \(2g - 1\), and the single literal that the third rod position has the rods’ type gives the same; (V) given R is false on both cut cylinders; on the grid of the bridgeheads of every cubic cell the parametrisation (C) is onto, so the real relaxation says nothing about the type set of a grid and the separability lemma has no product to act on; the weights of (C) seen along the rod axis return after \(\mathrm{lcm}(\gcd(a, c), \gcd(a, b))\) cubes, which is 4 on \((12, 8, 8)\) where (V) needs 15 grids; the \(g\) of the reach law is the modulus of the labels \(y - z\), \(y + z\) of the chains of the rod axis on the grids normal to it. Any proof of Theorem U must be integral from the first line. The branch certificates of the box lemma split into local ones, identical across \(b\), and global ones that span the closed circle and grow, with no rule found.
