theorem-h-orientation / thm-6-separability

Theorems 4 to 8 (line counts, two separable functions with product zero, walls, uniform chains beside a wall)

How the count of a type along a line depends on position: separable counts, walls, and uniform chains beside a wall.

In a frozen state the count of a type along a line is separable in the two transverse coordinates (Theorem 5); if every hole is oriented the count of a two-way type on a line depends on one transverse coordinate on each sublattice (Theorem 6); a \(k\)-free plane is a wall with one sign on the planes beside it (Theorem 7); the chains beside a wall are uniform (Theorem 8).
