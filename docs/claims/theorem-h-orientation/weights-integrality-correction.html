theorem-h-orientation / weights-integrality-correction

The weights of (C) are rarely integral (correction of 30 September 2026)

The gauge orbit of the chain weights contains an integer vector on 19 of the 637 odd frozen states of the cubic cell of side three, 20 of the 720 of side four, and none of 60 sampled solutions of the type model, with least denominators from 27 to 96 otherwise. The report of 10 September that says the weights can be taken integral on every state examined had examined those 19, and its height results are proved for integral weights only.
