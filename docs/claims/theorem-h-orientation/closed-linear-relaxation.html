theorem-h-orientation / closed-linear-relaxation

No weighted sum of laws proves (V) at \(g \geq 2\) (Theorem U section, items 3 to 5)

With the types relaxed to non-negative reals, the largest amount of the rods’ type on the plane of the bridgeheads is positive on every cell with \(g \geq 2\) (26/3, 178/9, 299/9 at cubic sides three, four, five), so the cage laws, which span the linear consequences of (T2), give no proof there; adding the signs, the bond rule and Lemma 3 lowers the optimum by less than one; on cells with coprime transverse extents the optimum is zero. Integrality of one family of indicators suffices, and case splits leave infeasible programmes, but no structure uniform in the side was found.
