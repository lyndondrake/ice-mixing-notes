theorem-h-orientation / parametrisation-c

The parametrisation (C) (completeness of the chain modes)

Every real solution of the type equations is a weighted sum of chain modes plus two constants.

Every real solution of the one-hot equations and (T2) on a cell is \(e_x\) plus a sum of chain modes (a weight on every chain of every axis) plus two antisymmetric constants: \(Y = F_x - F_z + \sigma\alpha\), \(Z = F_y - F_x + \sigma\beta\).
