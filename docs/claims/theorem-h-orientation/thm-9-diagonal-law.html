Theorem 9 (the certificate of the diagonal law, closed form)

For every odd separation \(s \geq 5\), the diagonal law on the causal diamond (Theorem 3) holds by an explicit linear certificate whose multipliers follow four rules in the position of each equation relative to the diamond.
