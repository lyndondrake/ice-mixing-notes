theorem-h-orientation / periodicity

Periodicity of frozen zero-flux states (first section, with Theorem P and Lemma A of the earlier note)

On every cell whose extents are all at least two, Theorem H implies that every frozen zero-flux state is invariant under a nonzero translation: if an axis is empty Lemma A gives the translation; otherwise zero flux makes every axis two-way, so the state has even curl by Theorem H, and Theorem P gives a one-cube period. So periodicity holds on the cubic cells of side one to eight (side one by direct computation), on the cells with a coprime pair of extents all at least two, on the cells with two chains in every pair of planes, and on the cells certified on 2026-10-01 with their divisors.
