theorem-h-orientation / kink-counts-per-class

The kink counts per chain class: the wave equation and one defect per axis (Theorem U section, item 10)

The counts of the rods’ type per chain class obey a discrete wave equation, and every chain of an axis has the same defect.

On a grid normal to the rod axis, with \(k[n][v]\) the number of vertices of the rods’ type in the class of value \(v\) (the trace of one chain of that axis), every real solution of (T2) satisfies the wave equation \(k[n+2][v] + k[n-2][v] = k[n][v+2] + k[n][v-2]\) (law C summed over the holes of a class), whose solutions are two sheets travelling one class per cube in opposite senses; and on the torus every chain of an axis has the same defect, the difference of its kink counts on its two grids, because the defect obeys a smoothing recurrence along the rod axis whose non-constant modes die round the circle.
