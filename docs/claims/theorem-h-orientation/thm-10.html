Theorem 10 (the coprime case of (V))

On a cell \((a, b, c)\) with \(\gcd(b, c) = 1\), a frozen state with an unoriented hole whose rods lie along \(x\) has even curl; so in an odd state every hole with rods along \(x\) is oriented.
